Electronic and Telecommunication Engineering

The Electronic and Telecommunication Engineering (ETE) program focuses on electronics, communication systems and modern telecommunication technologies.

About ETE

Students learn about electronic circuits, digital systems, signal processing, communication systems and telecommunication technologies.

Career Opportunities

Graduates can work in telecommunication companies, electronics industries, networking, communication systems and other technology-related organizations.
