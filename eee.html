Electrical and Electronic Engineering

The Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) program focuses on electrical systems, electronics, power systems and modern engineering technologies.

About EEE

Students learn about electrical circuits, electronics, power generation, control systems, communication systems and other important areas of electrical engineering.

Career Opportunities

Graduates can work in power and energy companies, electronics industries, telecommunication sectors, automation and other engineering organizations.
