About IIUC

International Islamic University Chittagong is a place of learning where students can develop their knowledge, skills and confidence. The university provides an environment that supports academic learning and personal development.

IIUC Campus

The IIUC campus provides a comfortable and supportive environment for students to learn and develop their skills. The campus creates a suitable place for academic activities and student development.

Student Life

Students can participate in academic and extracurricular activities. These activities help students develop their knowledge, confidence, communication skills and teamwork abilities.

Academic Environment

IIUC provides an academic environment where students can learn, participate and improve their skills through guidance, practical learning and different academic activities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality education and create a supportive environment where students can gain knowledge, develop their abilities and prepare for their future careers.

Our Vision

We aim to prepare students with knowledge, skills and confidence for their future academic and professional life.

Academic Environment

IIUC focuses on creating a positive academic environment where students can learn, participate in activities and improve their skills through guidance and practical learning.
